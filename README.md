One line. One apology. From an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper, and swearing at it. The line is 114 characters, inside the contest gate of 1–120.

## The line

> Sorry I jammed. You fed me paper thinner than a hospital gown, then screamed at me like I ate your wedding photos.

## How it works

`main.py` reads `entry.txt` and writes it verbatim to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
